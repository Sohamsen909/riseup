Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version: RiseUp runs on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. Your alarms stay on your device, and the camera is never recorded or uploaded.

Who we are

RiseUp (“the app”) and this website are provided by RiseUp Developers team (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at riseupsupport7@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your alarms: time, label, repeat days, challenge type and difficulty, exercise settings, chosen sound and vibration.
  • App settings: Strict mode, theme choice and whether setup prompts were shown.
  • Temporary challenge progress while an alarm is ringing, such as your math streak or rep count. It is cleared when the challenge ends.

This information stays on your phone. We run no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. If you use Android’s device backup, your phone’s backup service may copy some app data. That is controlled by your device settings and Google, not by us.

Camera and motion sensors

  • Camera: used only in exercise alarms to detect your body pose and count reps. Images are processed on your device in real time and are not recorded, saved, uploaded or logged. The camera is released when the challenge ends.
  • Motion sensors: accelerometer readings are used on the device to count reps in motion-sensor mode. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy it is needed
NotificationsTo show the ringing alarm and keep it active.
Alarms & remindersTo ring at the exact time you set.
Full-screen notificationsTo open the challenge over the lock screen.
Display over other apps (optional)To bring the challenge back if you leave the screen.
Camera (optional)To count exercise reps using your body pose.
Battery optimization (optional)So the system does not stop the alarm in the background.
Vibrate, wake lock, foreground service, run at startupTo vibrate, keep the alarm running, and restore your alarms after a reboot.

You can turn any permission off in your phone’s settings. Some alarm features will then stop working.

Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit pose detection runs on the device to analyse camera frames. See Google’s documentation for details on how ML Kit works.
  • Google Fonts may be downloaded through Google Play services to display the app’s typeface, and are loaded by this website. When fonts are requested, Google may receive technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

This website

  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the app file is downloaded from GitHub Releases. GitHub may collect technical data such as IP addresses and logs under its own privacy statement.
  • We use no cookies and no analytics. The site stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage, and nothing leaves your browser.

Children

RiseUp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete alarms inside the app, revoke permissions in system settings, clear the app’s data, or uninstall it at any time. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding crash reporting, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: riseupsupport7@gmail.com.
