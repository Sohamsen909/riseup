Questions

Is the camera recording me?

No. Pose detection runs on your phone. Nothing is saved, uploaded or logged. The camera is only used to count reps during an exercise alarm.

Why does Android warn me about this app?

This is a test build installed outside the Play Store, so Play Protect doesn’t recognise it. Only install APKs from sources you trust.

Can I still turn the alarm off in an emergency?

Yes. Power and phone calls are never blocked. If a sensor fails, a manual count option appears, and after 30 minutes the lock is released.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 and newer. Android 13 and above asks for notification permission, and Android 14 and above needs full-screen notifications allowed.

How do I update?

Download the newest APK from this page and install it over the old one. Your alarms are kept.
